Namespace Votanic.VXR2.Core.Locomotion

Classes

VXRBodyGroundPosition

Positions the XR Origin at a ground-aligned target while compensating for head offset. Used during teleportation to place the body at the destination.

VXRCameraForwardXZAlignment

Aligns the XR Origin forward direction on the XZ plane relative to the head controller. Used during teleportation to rotate the body around the head.

VXRHeadProvider

Locomotion provider that compensates for horizontal head movement by shifting the XR Origin. Used by VXRHeadController to keep the play space stable during head tracking.

VXRLocomotionWrapper

Central wrapper that wires vXR2 player controllers to XRI locomotion providers and input bindings.

VXRMoveProvider

Smooth locomotion provider for the vXR2 player. Moves the XR Origin from thumbstick input with optional head- or hand-relative direction.

VXRRecallProvider

Locomotion provider that instantly moves the XR Origin to a target transform. Used by RecallPlayers(Transform).

VXRRecallTransformation

Body transformation that snaps the XR Origin to a target transform's position and rotation.

VXRSnapTurnProvider

vXR2 snap turn provider based on XRI SnapTurnProvider. Rotates the rig in fixed left/right increments. Does not support 180° turn-around.

VXRTeleportationProvider

vXR2 teleportation provider that aligns body orientation and position using the head controller, and assigns itself to teleport anchors when the Teleport interactor hovers them.

VXRUpAlignment

Aligns the XR Origin up vector using the configured head controller context.
