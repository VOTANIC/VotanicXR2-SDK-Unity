Frequently Asked Questions

How to turn off "no cameras rendering" warning on my game view?

Go to Game tab > triple dot icon (on top right). Then uncheck Warn If No Cameras Rendering to turn off the warning.

How to connect to a head-mounted display (HMD)?

  1. Turn on and connect your HMD to the computer.
  2. Select VXR SDK Manager in the hierarchy.
  3. Go to VXR SDK Manager component and click Select Config. Then pick ConfigHMD.vxrc.

  1. Set the active control scheme to HMD.

  1. Play the scene and wear your HMD to see your project in HMD.
